# Pods Finance Yield Contracts

## Vaults

### Invariants
- **Invariant 1:** Users must go through one full round after the deposit has been made to be exposed to the strangle strategy.
  - **Significance:** Ensures that users cannot exploit the system by depositing and withdrawing within the same round to gain unfair advantages.
- **Invariant 2:** A user will always withdraw more or equal to what they have deposited, unless there are withdraw fees, negative rebasing events, or rounding errors.
  - **Significance:** Guarantees that users' principal is protected, and they receive their due returns unless specific conditions apply.
- **Invariant 3:** A user can always withdraw their principal after one week.
  - **Significance:** Ensures liquidity and accessibility for users, allowing them to withdraw their funds after a set period.
- **Invariant 4:** The vault's balance increases monotonically unless there are withdrawals or negative rebasing events.
  - **Significance:** Ensures the vault's balance reflects the correct state of deposits and withdrawals, maintaining integrity.
- **Invariant 5:** Processing deposits is invariant to the order and partition of the input.
  - **Significance:** Ensures that the order in which deposits are processed does not affect the final state, maintaining fairness and consistency.

### Ghost Variables
- **

:** 
  - **Role:** Indicates whether the vault is currently processing deposits.
  - **Testing:** Verify that deposits and withdrawals are only allowed when 

isProcessingDeposits

 is `false`.
- **`lastRoundAssets`:**
  - **Role:** Stores the assets at the end of the last round to calculate yield.
  - **Testing:** Ensure that `lastRoundAssets` is correctly updated at the end of each round and used in yield calculations.
- **

totalIdleAssets

:**
  - **Role:** Represents the total assets that are idle and not yet processed.
  - **Testing:** Check that 

totalIdleAssets

 accurately reflects the idle assets before and after processing deposits.

### Improvement Suggestions
- **Invariant:** Ensure that the vault's balance is correctly updated after each deposit and withdrawal.
  - **Significance:** Maintains the integrity of the vault's balance.
- **Ghost Variable:**

  - **Role:** Tracks the current round ID.

## Access Controls

### Invariants
- **Invariant 1:** The Multisig (VaultController) only has access to 50% of the weekly yield generated on the yield source, hence the Vault can only lose 50% of the yield generated by Lido.
  - **Significance:** Limits the risk exposure of the vault to the actions of the VaultController.
- **Invariant 2:** The Multisig (VaultController) cannot withdraw on behalf of other users.
  - **Significance:** Ensures that user funds are secure and cannot be accessed by the VaultController.
- **Invariant 3:** The Multisig (VaultController) cannot pause the system for more than 7 days.
  - **Significance:** Ensures that the system remains operational and accessible to users, preventing prolonged downtime.

### Ghost Variables

vaultController

  - **Testing:** Verify that only the VaultController can call functions like

 and 

startRound

.
- **`investorRatio`:**
  - **Role:** Determines the portion of yield transferred to the investor.
  - **Testing:** Ensure that the correct ratio of yield is transferred to the investor at the end of each round.

### Improvement Suggestions
- **Invariant:** Ensure that the VaultController cannot change critical parameters without proper authorization.
  - **Significance:** Prevents unauthorized changes to the vault's configuration.
- **Ghost Variable:** `withdrawFeeRatio`
  - **Role:** Represents the fee ratio for withdrawals.
  - **Testing:** Verify that the `withdrawFeeRatio` is correctly applied to withdrawals and does not exceed the maximum allowed value.
